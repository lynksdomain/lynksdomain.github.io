Three Pillars

Most apps make you choose: a music player, a reading app, a notes app. Termi is one session — music plays underneath while you read or write, and none of it asks you to leave.

MUSIC

Your own library, played back with a real reactive visualizer, generative album art, and a scrobbling option you control.

READING

A custom EPUB reader built from scratch — no web-view rendering, real terminal-styled typography, reading stats that actually mean something.

The Ritual

Modern apps optimize away every bit of friction — a "like" is as sterile as digital interaction gets. Old tech had ritual: mixtapes, modem handshakes, a CD passed hand to hand. Termi is built on the belief that some moments are better with a little weight to them, not less.

Termi Packages — a real send/receive ritual for gifting a mixtape, a book quote, or a piece of your own writing to one specific person. No feed, no followers, no server in the middle — just two people, present at the same time, sending something on purpose.

Why It Feels Different
  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into. Termi works the moment you open it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs. Not "opt-out" — genuinely absent from the app.
  • A real CRT aesthetic. Scanlines, phosphor glow, a proper boot sequence on every launch — not a filter, an actual rendering identity.
  • A custom tiling workspace on iPad. Not Apple's split view — a hand-built window manager with real panes, a floating content picker, and layouts that adapt to the device you're on.
  • An Apple Watch companion. Now Playing and remote control, styled like the rest of the app, not Apple's default Now Playing glance.
  • 15 themes, real reading fonts, and a one-time purchase. No subscription, ever — pay once for Lifetime Pro or don't pay at all.
Your Data, Your Device

Everything you put into Termi — your music, your books, your writing, your stats — is stored locally in the app's own sandbox. The only thing that ever leaves your device is your listening history, and only if you choose to connect a Last.fm account. Full details in the privacy policy.
